# FlowHRMS — Brand Guidelines

Version: 1.0  |  Date: 14 September 2026  |  Status: **In force** (supersedes the 07 August 2026 STF draft, kept under `docs/STF-Design-Handoff-v1/`)

## Brand hierarchy

**Flowacord** is the company and parent brand. **FlowHRMS** is its HRMS product. The product is always presented as a Flowacord product:

> **FlowHRMS** · by Flowacord

The company slogan is used wherever the company, not the product, is being introduced — the marketing footer, sign-in, email signatures:

> **ALIGN. ACCELERATE. GROW.**

Legal entity: Flowacord. Support: help@flowacord.com · +91 89088 88880. Product host: https://hrms.flowacord.com. Company site: https://flowacord.com.

## Assets — use, never redraw

The approved assets are the source of truth and are not reproduced by hand. In the repository:

| Asset | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Company logo, light surfaces | `public/brand/flowacord-logo-light.svg` | "Flow" in navy #34327F, "acord" in lavender |
| Company logo, dark surfaces | `public/brand/flowacord-logo-dark.svg` | "Flow" in paper #F5F5F5 |
| Icon mark | `public/brand/flowacord-mark.svg` | five dots + arrow; the app icon, favicon, avatar |
| Favicon | `public/brand/flowhrms-favicon.svg` | mark on transparent |
| App icon (PWA, Apple) | `public/brand/flowhrms-app-icon.svg` and `/icons/*`, `/apple-icon` | mark on the brand ground #010123 |

The **FlowHRMS wordmark** is derived, not drawn: `src/components/brand/FlowHRMSWordmark.tsx` is generated by `scripts/build-wordmark.mjs` from the official logo. It keeps the icon mark and the outlined "Flow" glyphs with their coordinates untouched, drops "acord", and sets **HRMS** in the brand's secondary face on the same baseline, at the cap height of the original, with a small gap after the "w". `FlowHRMSLockup` adds the "by Flowacord" line. `FlowacordMark` renders the icon mark alone.

Do not: stretch, recolour, outline, add effects to, or place the mark on a busy photograph. Do not set "FlowHRMS" in any other face as a substitute for the wordmark.

## Typography

- **Primary face: Neulis Alt** — used in the company logo only. It is a licensed face and is not shipped; the logo is outlined vector.
- **Secondary face: Poppins** — headings (600/700), display type on marketing, and "HRMS" in the wordmark (SemiBold Italic). Self-hosted through `next/font`.
- **Body: Wix Madefor Text** (unchanged) — dense tables and long forms read better in it at small sizes than Poppins does.
- **Figures: Poppins** — numbers, times, codes and small meta lines (`font-mono` in code). Replaced Spline Sans Mono on 27 Sept 2026 at the owner's request.

## Voice

Unchanged from the founding brief, because it was never about the name: short active sentences, consequences before actions, errors that say what happened and what to do next. Practical, calm, accountable. No surveillance language, no empty enterprise jargon. Payroll never claims statutory compliance.

## Where the brand appears

Browser title "FlowHRMS by Flowacord" and "%s · FlowHRMS"; PWA name "FlowHRMS"; sign-in and auth screens carry the lockup; the app shell carries the wordmark (mark alone when collapsed); the marketing site carries the lockup, the slogan and the support contacts in its footer; invitation and enquiry emails say FlowHRMS; the share card says "FlowHRMS · by Flowacord".
